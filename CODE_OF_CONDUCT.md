# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

OfficeOS is built by people working across infrastructure, developer tools, and AI systems. We expect contributors to keep discussions technical, direct, and respectful.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be clear about tradeoffs, risks, and assumptions.
- Treat bug reports, reviews, and support requests as shared engineering work.
- Give actionable feedback that can be evaluated or implemented.
- Respect different levels of context and experience.
- Keep disagreements focused on the design, code, or operational impact.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Dismissing security, safety, or abuse concerns without review.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other moderation actions when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

If you need to report a conduct issue, use GitHub's report content flow or contact the repository maintainers privately. For security vulnerabilities, follow the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports should include links, screenshots, usernames, dates, and any other context that helps maintainers understand what happened. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and avoid sharing reporter details beyond what is needed to resolve the issue.
